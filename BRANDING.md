# Gloria Esser Teaching Tool — Branding & Dedication

**Public product name:** Gloria Esser Teaching Tool

**Tagline:** Remember Why You Teach.

**Dedication:** In honor of Gloria Esser's 50 years devoted to teaching. Created to give teachers more time for what matters: inspiring students, building relationships, and helping children succeed.

**Short iPhone home-screen name:** Gloria Esser

**App Store name:** Gloria Esser Teaching Tool (subject to availability and Apple's review).

**Brand promise:** Technology handles repetitive work so the teacher can focus on the human side of education. No attempt to replace teacher judgment, autonomy, or relationships.
