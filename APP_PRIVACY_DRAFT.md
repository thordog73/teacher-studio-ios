# Gloria Esser Teaching Tool — Privacy Policy Draft (requires review and a public hosted URL)

This draft describes the **TestFlight development prototype**. It is **not** a final legal policy for a production school/family app.

**What is stored locally:** Teacher-authored teaching resources, personal files, local classroom drafts and fictional demonstration records may be saved on the device using local browser/app storage. The app's personal backup feature encrypts selected teacher-owned materials with a passphrase before export. The developer does not receive those exports automatically.

**Optional online test service:** If a tester chooses to connect a separate Supabase development project, the service may process the adult's login email address, test classroom entries, assignments and messages created by testers. The project administrator must disclose the provider, retention, access rights and deletion process before use. Online communication is *not* active by default.

**Children's data:** The current build is limited to fictional names and test content. It must not be used to collect real students' grades, scans, images or other education records. A later school-authorized service will require separate terms, guardian verification, policy/FERPA/COPPA review as applicable, and stronger security measures.

**Third-party infrastructure:** Offline teaching tools do not call developer servers or an AI model. If an optional Supabase project is used, identity/transport services are provided by that separately configured provider. Apple may independently receive device analytics under users' Apple settings, which requires careful public disclosure.

**Data deletion:** Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app data. Explicitly saved external encrypted backups are under the user's control and must be deleted from the destination separately. Before public release, add clear in-app controls for any connected account deletion and server-stored information.

**Contact & effective date:** Add the developer's verified contact information and effective date before hosting this page. Do not publish until fact-checked against final functionality and App Store App Privacy answers.
